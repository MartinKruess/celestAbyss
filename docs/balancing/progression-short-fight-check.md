# Gegenprobe: Dauer-DPS und frische Fünf-Sekunden-Kämpfe

- Same builds, costs, class caps, buff providers and armor as the600-second calibration. Normal, non-attacking dummy; no passive HP regeneration or outside healing.
- Five-second HP reference=(mean damage in the first5seconds+median damage in the first5seconds)/2 across eleven damage classes. This is separate from sustained DPS times5.
- A fresh fight starts with full resources and prepared buffs. All cooldowns start in the same state as in the source calibration; skill hits at the5-second boundary count.
- One common HP cannot enforce a narrow kill-time band for all classes. Report actual kill times and controls instead of assuming it.
- The20/30 full-skill upper bounds are not progression baselines. Level120 reuses R6 gear/stones. Undefined item upgrades and other future bonuses remain excluded.
